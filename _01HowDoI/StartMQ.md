Skyrim Unification Project utilizes <a href="https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/52704" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">At Your Own Pace</a> to adjust the progression of the main quest.

To progress to Bleak Falls Barrow, the dragon attack, or the Blades quest, talk to Farengar at Dragonsreach.
